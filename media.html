Latest episode

Molecular fingerprints

The one-in-a-billion whisper of Raman scattering

What if a tiny whisper of scattered light could reveal the molecular identity of a cell, a virus or a new material? Almost every photon that hits a molecule bounces off unchanged. About one in a billion does not — and that one carries a fingerprint.

The episode covers where the effect comes from, why it went almost unused for decades until lasers arrived, how plasmonic surfaces made it practical, and where it is heading in graphene characterisation, molecular sensing and healthcare diagnostics.
